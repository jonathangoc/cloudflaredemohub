# 05: Wrap Up
## Key takeaways

You leave this lab ready to explain, demonstrate, and position Cloudflare's approach to securing agentic AI. Using the Seven Hills application, you follow real traffic across the public ingress, model, and MCP tool paths, applying identity, DLP, guardrails, dynamic routing, cost controls, and threat detection. You validate both sides of the customer outcome: malicious or unauthorized activity is blocked, while legitimate customer requests and business workflows continue to work.

You can now turn that technical experience into a clear customer story and repeatable demonstration. Cloudflare's differentiation is its ability to apply security, connectivity, and AI controls across the full application path through one integrated platform, with consistent policy and visibility and minimal application changes. You should leave confident discussing the architecture, demonstrating business value, and showing customers why consolidating these controls is simpler than operating multiple point products.
